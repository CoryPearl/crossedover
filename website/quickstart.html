CROSSEDOVER · V1.0.0

Get started.

A few minutes of setup. Then, your Windows Steam library.

Before you start

You need an Apple silicon Mac, macOS 15 or 26, Rosetta 2, an internet connection, and your own Steam account. Allow 8 GiB free for setup, plus space for your games.

1. Make it at home

  1. Download crossedover v1.0.0 and open the DMG.
  2. Drag crossedover into Applications.
  3. Eject the disk image and open crossedover from Applications.

First open

This release is locally signed and not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it and you trust this download, try opening it once, then use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Confirm the app name. Follow Apple’s instructions; do not disable Gatekeeper system-wide.

If macOS offers to install Rosetta 2, follow Apple’s prompt.

2. Bring in Steam

  1. Choose Set up Steam. The app prepares its included runtime and downloads Windows Steam directly from Valve. Allow a few minutes.
  2. Sign in inside Steam’s window, completing Steam Guard if requested.
  3. Install a Windows game through that Steam client.

Your native Mac Steam installation stays separate. No accounts, game files, or saved games are bundled with crossedover.

3. Pick something to play

Completed installs appear automatically. Press Play to launch, ⌘R to refresh, or ⌘F to search. The grid/list selector remembers your preference. If the app requests a graphics-profile restart, close your current game first.

Use ⋯ for properties, files, verification, and uninstall. Steam handles uninstall confirmations. Settings → Windows Steam → Open download folder opens installed games in Finder. The separate Steam Downloads button opens download progress and updates.

Know what’s experimental

TrackMania Nations Forever is the first tested demo and uses Performance graphics. CS2 uses Metal; its native window controls and match performance are still being validated. Other titles are untested. Compatibility, performance, and long-session stability vary.

Choose your graphics

Settings explains each profile. Legacy uses OpenGL for older DirectX 9 games and serves as a compatibility fallback. Performance uses DXVK and Metal for DirectX 9, including TrackMania Nations Forever. Metal uses DXMT for DirectX 10–11, including CS2. Quit Steam and all games before changing profiles.

Your files

The app is in /Applications/crossedover.app. Games, runtimes, saves, Windows Steam, and backups live separately under:

~/Library/Application Support/CrossedOver/

Use Open download folder to browse your games, or ⋯ → Show game files for one game. Full environment backups are manual only and include all installed games. Small copies of changed settings are kept for recovery. Settings → Clear backups moves old full backups to Trash while keeping current games and saves. Empty Trash in Finder to reclaim space; APFS clones can share blocks.

Uninstall everything

Quit Steam and all games, then choose Settings → Uninstall crossedover → Uninstall app and data. This moves the app, its Windows Steam client, managed games, local saves, runtimes, and backups to Trash, clears preferences, and quits. Export local saves you want to keep first, then empty Trash to reclaim space. Your separate Mac Steam library is unaffected.

Dragging only the app to Trash leaves its separate data folder behind. Use the in-app uninstaller for complete removal. Custom data locations and apps opened from a read-only DMG must be managed in Finder.

Coming soon

More than Steam games is coming soon: other games, launchers, and platforms are planned. v1.0.0 focuses on Windows Steam.

Need a hand?

  • Missing game: wait for installation to finish, then press ⌘R.
  • Slow first launch: check Steam for a prerequisite or setup dialog.
  • Frozen game: try ⌘Tab and quit normally. Recovery tools are in Settings → Advanced. Force quit can lose unsaved progress.
  • Reporting a problem: save a diagnostic report, review it, and open an issue. Never share passwords or Steam Guard codes.
